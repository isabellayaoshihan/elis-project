09  DEFINITIONS AND REPRODUCIBILITY
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  BLS footprint: unique valid-format county FIPS present in the BLS source during at
  least one year, excluding Puerto Rico. This is not an official count of counties
  in any single year.
  Absent key: a county-year-measure combination without a structurally valid M13
  record. Unusable rate: an absent key or one with missing, invalid or duplicate
  numeric data.
  Value classes are mutually exclusive: blank/null, recognized missing marker, other
  nonnumeric text, or numeric. Flags in other categories may overlap. Arithmetic
  tolerances do not prove source accuracy.
  Raw data were read only. ACS contributed a FIPS reference list, not a joined
  table. No original observations were removed, filled or rewritten.
